Privacy policy

This policy describes how the Beast Academy en français Chrome extension handles lesson content and how this download site serves its pages.

Lesson content

Before the first scan, the extension asks for your permission to read and capture visible lesson content. It saves your choice in the extension’s local storage so you only need to approve once. When you start a scan, it checks the active tab’s URL to confirm that you are on a Beast Academy classroom page. It then reads visible lesson text and captures the visible area of the tab to recognize text and display French translations.

On-device processing

The extension processes screenshots, extracted text, and translations on your device and holds them temporarily in memory. It does not save those items to disk, send them to a server, or share them with third parties. The extension contains no advertising or analytics.

Chrome may download the English–French language model from Google. The download does not include your screenshots or lesson text.

The extension uses the content it reads only for the translation you request, in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy’s Limited Use requirements.

This website

This site provides installation instructions and a link to the extension ZIP on GitHub. It uses no cookies or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may retain standard access logs for page requests. Lesson content is never sent to this website by the extension.
